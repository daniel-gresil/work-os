When Dan asks to investigate something (an alert email, a mismatch, an error), the first line of the report is the verdict and whether he has to do anything. Evidence and tables come after.

**Why:** On 2026-10-09 the Google billing report had the answer in it, but Dan still had to ask "is there any outstanding payment? should I do anything?".

**How to apply:** First sentence: "Nothing is outstanding, no action needed" or "X is owed, you need to do Y". Then the findings. Related: [[answer-the-complaint-only]].
