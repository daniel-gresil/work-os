When Dan approves a step, carry out the clear-cut parts straight away and bring back only the items that need a real decision (duplicates, unsaved work, anything irreversible). Prefer recoverable actions: move to Trash rather than delete.

**Why:** In the work-os build (2026-10-01) he answered "Approve" or "agreed" to recommendations and expected the work to proceed, while deciding duplicates and deletions himself, item by item.

**How to apply:** After an approval, act on everything unambiguous, then list what was held back and why. See [[ask-questions-one-at-a-time]] for how to bring the held items back.
