When the requester has answered a question that was open on an ADO card (in the task doc's communication log or an email), write the answer on the card as a rule. Do not add a "to confirm" marker or propose asking again.

**Why:** On 2026-10-09 I marked Ody's 1X answer ("1X is an XL printed as 1X") as still to confirm; Dan pointed out it already said where the pieces go.

**How to apply:** Read the answer for what it implies, state the implication as the rule, and only re-ask when two readings lead to different builds. See [[ask-questions-one-at-a-time]].
